## 1. Purpose and Use

The secretary of each governance body (GOV-04) — Steering Committee, Product Council, Architecture Review Board, Change Control Board, Data & AI Ethics Board, Process-Performance Review — minutes every meeting with this form. Decisions flow to DAR-02 or CM-03; actions flow to the RAID log (RSK-03).

*Guidance: minutes record decisions and actions, not a transcript. Circulate within two working days. Delete guidance lines before use.*
