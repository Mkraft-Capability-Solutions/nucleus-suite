## 1. Purpose and Use

Status is reported at the cadence of MC-01: workstreams weekly, the programme fortnightly and the Steering Committee monthly. Measures come from MPM-01; predictions from PPM-01 and PPM-02 are shown beside the objective they serve.

*Guidance: RAG rules — Green: within tolerance. Amber: tolerance breached but recoverable within the period with a plan. Red: needs escalation or a decision. Delete guidance lines before use.*
