## 1. Purpose and Scope

This strategy covers the **people side** of adopting Nucleus Suite. Its aim is that every persona (GOV-08) moves from awareness to sustained, proficient use across delivery phases P0–P5.

It is distinct from **configuration change control** (CM-02), which governs changes to baselined artefacts.

Scope:

- process, role and behaviour changes in HR, payroll, plant and time-office operations, line management and employee self-service;
- adoption of AI copilots and agents;
- frontline and contract-worker enablement.

The reference rollout context is the fictional pilot tenant **Vindhya Precision Industries Pvt Ltd** (2 legal entities, 5 sites, 4 states), used in phase P2 Pilot & Intelligence.

## 2. Change Vision

> "Every worker — from the CHRO's office to the night shift on the plant floor — can do their HR tasks in their own language, on the device they have, in seconds, and trusts that the system is fair, private and correct."

**Case for change (by audience):**

| Audience | What's in it for me |
| :--- | :--- |
| HR Ops / HRBP | Fewer manual tickets; effective-dated changes without spreadsheets; AI drafts |
| Payroll / Finance | Controlled 8-stage payroll; explainable variances; balanced ERP postings |
| Plant supervisors | Live shift board; one-tap approvals; registers always inspection-ready |
| Line managers | One inbox for approvals; team coverage visible; faster 1:1s |
| Employees | Self-service by voice, app or business messaging; transparent payslips; career growth |
| Contract workers | Proof of hours and overtime; payslips in their language; grievance channel |
| Leadership | Real-time workforce insight; scenario planning; compliance assurance |

## 4. Change Readiness Assessment

**Method:** survey plus focus groups per stakeholder group before G1, repeated before each pilot / go-live.

| Dimension | Indicators | Scale |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Leadership sponsorship | Visible sponsor messages; leaders using S1/S7 consoles | 1–5 |
| Change history | Outcomes of past system rollouts; change fatigue | 1–5 |
| Digital readiness | Device access, literacy, connectivity by site | 1–5 |
| Process maturity | Documented processes; data quality baseline | 1–5 |
| Capacity | Availability of super-users; overlap with peak periods (year-end, appraisal cycle) | 1–5 |
| Trust in AI and data use | Attitudes to monitoring, AI recommendations, privacy | 1–5 |

**Go / no-go input:** an average below 3 for any group with **H** impact triggers a mitigation plan, recorded in RSK-02, before go-live.

## 5. ADKAR-Based Approach

| ADKAR stage | Objective | Interventions | Owner | Measured by |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Awareness** | Why the change is needed | Sponsor town halls; CHRO video; plant huddles; union briefing; FAQ (GOV-09 C-08..C-14) | Sponsor, OCM Lead | Awareness pulse ≥ 85% |
| **Desire** | Personal motivation to take part | What's-in-it-for-me stories (§2); champions' demos; early wins at the Vindhya pilot | Champions, line managers | Sentiment ≥ 3.5 / 5 |
| **Knowledge** | How to use the new processes and tools | Role-based training (OCM-02); in-app guides; Nucleus Talk tutorials | OCM Lead, L&D | Assessment pass ≥ 80% |
| **Ability** | Doing it in real work | Sandbox practice; floor walkers; hypercare; job aids | Super-users | Task success; error rates |
| **Reinforcement** | Sustained use | Adoption dashboards; recognition; retire legacy tools; performance goals for managers | Line managers, HR leadership | KPIs in §9 sustained 3 months |

## 6. Change Network

| Role | Ratio / coverage | Responsibilities | Time commitment |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Executive sponsor | 1 | Visible leadership; removes blockers | Monthly |
| Change leads (per business unit / legal entity) | 1 per BU | Local planning; readiness tracking | 20% |
| HR champions | 1 per 10 HR staff | Process expertise; peer coaching | 10–15% |
| **Plant floor champions** | 1 per shift per site (e.g. 5 Vindhya sites × shifts) | Shift-start demos; kiosk help; feedback capture in local language | 1 h per shift during rollout |
| Manager champions | 1 per 50 managers | Approval and feedback practices | 5% |
| Contractor liaisons | 1 per contractor firm | Contract-worker onboarding; wage-query triage | As needed |
| Super-users | 1 per 25 users in high-impact groups | First-line support during hypercare | 20% during hypercare |

Champions receive early access, train-the-trainer sessions (OCM-02) and a feedback channel straight to the Product Council.

## 7. Resistance Management

| Likely resistance | Root cause | Response |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| "Biometric / geofence is surveillance" | Privacy fear; past misuse | Publish the monitoring policy (co-reviewed with the union); show what is and is not collected (GOV-10); no covert monitoring (GOV-11 §4) |
| "AI will decide my rating / reject candidates" | Fear of automation | Explain HITL and tiers; show explanations; human-review route (GOV-11 §7) |
| Supervisors: "extra admin on the floor" | Workload | Voice and one-tap flows; measure and publish time saved |
| HR: "I lose my spreadsheets" | Loss of control | Exports with governance; analytics in S2; involve them in report design |
| Payroll: "risky to switch mid-year" | Accuracy risk | Parallel runs; phased cut-over; explicit reconciliation sign-off |
| Low digital literacy | Skills / access | Kiosks, voice-first, business messaging, pictorial guides, assisted mode |

Resistance hotspots are tracked as risks in RSK-02 with owners and reviewed fortnightly.

## 8. Union and Works Council Engagement

1. Early briefing before G1 on scope: time and attendance, biometrics, overtime, AI uses, data retention.
2. Joint review of:
   - the workforce monitoring and biometric policy;
   - the AI prohibited-uses list and HITL commitments;
   - wage and overtime transparency features for contract workers.
3. Formal consultation where required by law, agreements or standing orders — **to be validated by Legal** per tenant and state.
4. Signed minutes stored under configuration control; commitments traced to requirements (REQ-02).
5. Joint grievance review during hypercare (first 60 days after go-live).

### 9.1 Quantitative Measure Specifications (Input to Process-Performance Baselines, MPM-01)

BG-09 targets CMMI Maturity Level 5. Adoption measures are therefore operationally defined so that they can be statistically baselined, controlled and improved.

**Rules:**

- Each measure is registered in the MPM-01 measure catalogue under the ID below.
- A process-performance baseline (PPB) is set once at least **20 subgroups** are available. Typical subgroups are site × week or persona × week; the Vindhya pilot provides the first data set.
- Points outside control limits, or runs of 8 or more points on one side of the centre line, trigger causal analysis (CAR).

| Measure ID | Operational definition (numerator / denominator) | Unit | Subgroup & frequency | Data source | Control chart | Spec / target | Process-performance model (PPM) use |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| MEA-OCM-01 Active usage rate | Distinct users with ≥ 1 completed transaction in the week / licensed active users in the persona | % | Persona × site × week | Product analytics (anonymised) | p-chart | ≥ 85% employees; ≥ 95% managers / HR | Predicts helpdesk load (MEA-OCM-05) |
| MEA-OCM-02 Self-service rate | Transactions initiated by the subject employee or manager / all HR transactions of eligible types | % | Site × week | Workflow audit trail | p-chart | ≥ 80% | Input to HR-effort savings model |
| MEA-OCM-03 Digital punch coverage | Punches via biometric / geofence / kiosk / app / business messaging / voice / all punches (manual entries in denominator) | % | Site × shift × day | TIM punch store | p-chart | ≥ 98% | Predicts regularisation volume and payroll-input timeliness |
| MEA-OCM-04 Approval cycle time | Hours from submission to final approval, per request (working-calendar adjusted) | hours | Request type × week | Workflow engine | X̄-S chart (log-transformed) | Median ≤ 24 h; 90th percentile ≤ 48 h | SLA-breach prediction |
| MEA-OCM-05 How-to ticket rate | "How-do-I" tickets / 100 active users | tickets per 100 users | Site × week | SSV helpdesk (categorised) | u-chart | ≤ 5 by day 90; declining trend | Training effectiveness feedback (MEA-TRN-04) |
| MEA-OCM-06 Agent confirmation-acceptance rate | Confirmed agent write previews / write previews shown | % | Agent × week | INT action ledger | p-chart | ≥ 85% | Intent-model quality (MEA-AI-05) |
| MEA-OCM-07 Readiness index | Mean of §4 dimensions (1–5) per stakeholder group | score | Group × assessment wave | Readiness survey | Individuals (I-MR) chart across waves | ≥ 3.0 for H-impact groups before go-live | Predicts post-go-live adoption (regression on MEA-OCM-01) |
| MEA-OCM-08 Sentiment / trust score | Mean pulse score (ease, trust in data use, trust in AI) | score 1–5 | Persona × month | Pulse survey (n ≥ 30 per subgroup) | X̄-S chart | ≥ 3.8 | Leading indicator for resistance risks (RSK-02) |

Statistical methods, data-quality checks and PPB/PPM ownership follow MPM-01. Survey subgroups with fewer than 5 respondents are suppressed (GOV-10 §13).

## 10. Go-Live Hypercare

| Element | Approach |
| :--- | :--- |
| Duration | 4–6 weeks per go-live; payroll hypercare spans at least 2 payroll cycles |
| Command centre | Daily stand-up of Product, Support, OCM, Payroll and Plant leads; issue triage within 2 h |
| Floor walkers | Super-users at each site for the first 2 weeks of each shift pattern |
| Support | Tiered: champion → SSV helpdesk → product team; business messaging / voice help where enabled |
| Communications | Daily hypercare status bulletin; fixes announced in-app |
| Exit criteria | Severity 1/2 issues closed; adoption KPIs on trend; payroll reconciled; hand-over to business-as-usual support |

## 11. Building Trust in AI Adoption

1. **Start with Tier 1–2:** the Policy Assistant and drafting tools come first to build familiarity; Tier 3–4 features are introduced only after trust metrics are positive.
2. **Show the work:** citations, explanations and the user's own action-ledger view.
3. **Always a human route:** visible "talk to HR" and "request human review" paths.
4. **Co-design:** champions and union representatives test AI features in the pilot.
5. **Publish commitments:** a plain-language AI charter summarising GOV-11 principles and prohibited uses, in all enabled languages.
6. **Measure trust:** pulse items on AI trust; override and rejection rates reviewed with the DAEB.

## 12. Sustainment

- Change ownership moves to business-unit HR leadership after hypercare exit.
- Quarterly adoption reviews feed the product backlog.
- New-joiner onboarding includes Nucleus Suite enablement.
- The champion network stays in place for each phase (P3 Depth, P4 Scale, P5 Frontier features).
- Lessons learned captured using the lessons-learned record (PCM-01) at G5.
